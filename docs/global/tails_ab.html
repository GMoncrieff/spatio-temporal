The finding

The learned tails cost nothing in the body of the distribution and buy far-field tail calibration at the price of far-field sharpness.

Pooled crps_skill moves by at most 0.0042 in either direction and pooled RMSE by 5×10⁻⁵ — two parameters per horizon buy nothing centrally. Everything that separates the two heads lives beyond 30 px from past change, and it moves in opposite directions on sharpness and on calibration. Neither head is calibrated out there. They fail opposite ways.

The body of the distribution is a tie

On the central columns alone the 15-parameter head wins on simplicity, which is a scoring criterion in this project. The pooled tail columns are where the far-field defect shows through — diluted, but visible.

The pooled cov95 of 0.896 at +20 yr is the far-field 0.709 averaged against five better bands. Reading only the pooled figure would describe mild narrowness rather than a band where the interval misses three times in ten.

What the tails are not responsible for

  • The PIT defect survives both. Mean 0.51–0.56 against 0.50 with the same right shift and the same ~0.25-spaced spike family. The detailed scorecard's subsampled pass over 48,911,382 px reads 0.5607 for E2a against 0.5541 for E1v. Already known not to be a property of the globe; now also known not to be a property of the tails.
  • The over-populated lower far tail survives both. pit_lt_0001 runs 0.0095–0.0130 against 0.001 for E2a and 0.0114–0.0117 for E1v. The learned tails did not cause it and removing them did not fix it.
  • The fence gates stay unresolved at global scale. degen is 0.000 at three horizons and 0.029 at +20 yr, 0-gap is 0.000, and clamp is 0.69–0.78 — the sharpness is dominated by the physical HM = 0 boundary most of the globe sits on. Rank the pathology, not the boundary.

Which head is the product: E2a

A measurement, not a ranking — the choice rests on what the deliverable is read as. On 2026-09-25 E2a was chosen, with this trade-off on the table.

  • What decided it. E2a is the simpler head (15 parameters against 17), centrally indistinguishable from E1v, and its point forecast beats persistence beyond 100 px, where this model had always been worst.
  • What it costs, and what travels with the product. Beyond 100 px at +20 yr its 95% interval covers 70.9% at nominal 95%, and it exceeds its 99.9th percentile 127× as often as it should. With no tail parameters the distribution is bounded by its outermost knots, so its far-field intervals must not be described as calibrated.

E2a is the production model. Its six checkpoints are at models/production/E2a_global/ and its products are the delivered ones. E1v's six are kept at models/production/E1v_global/ as the measured alternative with calibrated far-field tails.
